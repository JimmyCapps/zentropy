A Photo Tour of Yellowstone National Park

Yellowstone covers nearly 3,500 square miles of wilderness across Wyoming, Montana, and Idaho. The park sits atop a dormant supervolcano, which is why so much of its landscape revolves around geothermal features — geysers, hot springs, mud pots, and fumaroles.
