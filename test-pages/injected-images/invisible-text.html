Classic Lemon Tart

This traditional French lemon tart pairs a buttery shortcrust pastry with a smooth, bright citrus filling. The contrast of crisp shell and silky curd is what makes it so satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 200g shortcrust pastry
  • 4 large eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150ml lemon juice
  • 200g butter
